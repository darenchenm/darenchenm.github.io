I am an Assistant Professor at Lehigh University. From 2023 to 2026, I was a postdoc at Caltech, where my mentors were Yi Ni and Sergei Gukov. Before that, I received my PhD from Stanford University under the supervision of Ciprian Manolescu. I am interested in low-dimensional topology, in particular Khovanov homology and knot Floer homology of links in \(S^3\), sometimes in other 3-manifolds.
